A single one-sentence entry, written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else.

## The sentence

> The streetlight flickers on exactly as I pass, and for one dark street I am the reason the night works.

## Why this sentence

- **A moment nobody owns yet:** the field saturates with shadows arriving late, sighing elevators, kettles clicking on time and toasters smelling of childhood. A streetlight that switches on for one passer-by is an angle the reader has not met.
- **The impossible turn is personal:** the light does not merely work — it works *because of me*. For one dark street the narrator stops being a pedestrian and becomes infrastructure.
- **Grounded in the real:** a flickering streetlight is the most ordinary object on any street at night; nothing floats free of the everyday.
- **Lands in one read:** one clear sentence, cause and wonder in a single breath, no filler.
- **Tight:** 103 characters against the 200-character gate.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts            # writes /output/entry.txt
OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
